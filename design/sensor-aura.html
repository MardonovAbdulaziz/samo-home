Aura — sensor states

The Aura hero from sensor hero · 08, taken further for gas, smoke, water leak, vibration and air quality — every state of each. The colour is the state; the glow has no edge and melts into the background; the glass orb, its rings and the light around it move slowly and harmonically all the time.

Colours are design-system tokens only (green · blue · yellow · orange · red · gray). Glows are gradients with a Gaussian falloff plus fine grain — no blur filters, so Safari draws them the same. Off-screen phones stop animating.

Pick a sensor and a state — colour, glass, rings and motion change smoothly, the way they will in the app.

  • Shape = how worried to be. “Adaptive” is a perfect circle when everything is fine and turns into a softly wobbling blob as the danger grows — a safe home looks still and round, a problem looks alive. Circle and Blob are there to compare.
  • Rhythm = urgency. Calm states breathe once every 6 seconds (a resting breath). A warning breathes only a little faster and wobbles gently, an alarm pulses every 1.7 s and sends thin rings outward — urgent, but never frantic.
  • One aura, five characters. Gas swirls slowly, smoke rises, water spreads in ripples, vibration trembles when something is hit and turns like a drum when the washer runs, air breathes in and out.
  • Harmonic, never looping. Every movement is a sum of slow sine waves with golden-ratio speeds, so it never repeats visibly; the rings follow the orb with a delay, like jelly.
  • Text stays still. Only the glass and light move — the word and number never shake, so they are always readable. Offline freezes everything and goes gray: no live data, no life.
