Sensor hero — 10 variants

The top of every sensor page: the 1–3 values that matter most. Each variant is shown on three different sensors — a number sensor (temperature & humidity), a multi-value sensor (air quality) and a state sensor (water leak) — so we can pick one style that also fits motion, presence, smoke, gas / CO, vibration, energy meters and valves later.

Real app colours, font and icons. Values are the last report; "Reported 2 min ago" stays under the hero in every variant. Cards under the hero are the same in all variants.
