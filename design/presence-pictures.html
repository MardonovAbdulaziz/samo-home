Presence sensor — new picture

The old room plan (grid, cone, walls) is replaced by something everyone reads at a glance: a person and the furniture they use. The radar's strength is that it sees people who sit still, read, work or sleep — so the picture shows exactly that: an empty chair, someone sitting in it, someone walking past.

Colours: furniture = door-frame graphite (like the door / window sensor), person = app blue (= the blue “Someone here” word). Breathing when still, steps when walking, a fading ghost while “Leaving…”, faded scene when offline. Only transform and opacity animate; no blur.

01

Furniture — “Display as”

My pick

One piece of furniture in profile, chosen in settings like the door sensor's “Display as”: Armchair (living room), Bed (bedroom — the person lies under a blanket and breathes), Desk (office). Empty = the empty chair. Someone walking = a person walking past it. Sitting still = sitting in it, breathing.

02

Room in profile — places

For sensors with zones (FP1, FP2): the room as a simple side view — sofa, desk, door — and the person moves to the place the sensor reports: sits on the sofa, sits at the desk, stands in the door. No grid, no metres.

03

Sensor mark — person

The same 100 px mark the leak, smoke and gas sensors use, with the person icon. Blue disc + a soft breathing ring when someone is here. The simplest, but says nothing about sitting or walking.

04

Sensor + person

The white sensor on the wall, like the motion sensor's picture, and the person standing in front of it. Soft blue light falls on the person when seen. Shows “a device that watches the room”, but less about what the person is doing.
