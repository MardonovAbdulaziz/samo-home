Rules

Light means “on”Glow only on things that are on, chosen or need you. Everything else stays dark and calm.
Edges, not outlinesA fine light edge on the top-left, the blue rising from the edges in — never a hard border all around.
FastOnly gradients and shadows. No live blur under tiles and cards — the bars and sheets keep the real glass.
